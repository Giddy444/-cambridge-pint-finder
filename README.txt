Where We Going Then? V9.9 — Cromer

Adds Cromer to the V9.8 app with 20 currently usable CAMRA-listed venues. Bouncers was omitted because current business data marks it permanently closed. Cromer is included in city selection, map, Near Me city detection, wheel and Pub Crawl. Pub Crawl now works even when beer prices have not yet been entered.

Note: Cromer pin positions are street-level starter coordinates and should be verified before a public launch.
